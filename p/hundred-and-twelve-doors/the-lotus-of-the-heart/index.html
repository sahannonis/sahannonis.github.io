The Lotus of the Heart

The Lotus of the Heart: a meditation from verse 49 of the Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra. You gather all of yourself, attention, senses, feeling, into the space at the centre of the chest, pictured as a lotus with space at its centre, and you stay there. Step by step, with why it works, in The Book of a Hundred and Twelve Doors by S. Nonis.
