Dappled Light

Dappled Light: a meditation from verse 76 of the Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra. Sunlight through leaves, a lamp's glow on a wall, light on water: the verse asks you to look at space filled with patches of light, and let your attention rest in the light itself. Step by step, with why it works, in The Book of a Hundred and Twelve Doors by S. Nonis.
