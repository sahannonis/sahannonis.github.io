The Wave Is the Sea

The Wave Is the Sea: a meditation from verse 98 of the Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra. Where door 73 lets a desire fall, this one turns towards it: you take the desire, or the thought, as a wave of your own awareness, and look into it until you see the awareness in it. Step by step, with why it works, in The Book of a Hundred and Twelve Doors by S. Nonis.
