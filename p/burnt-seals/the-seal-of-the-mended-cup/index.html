The Seal of the Mended Cup

The Seal of the Mended Cup: a sigil drawn from the words “I carry my grief gently.” In the Japanese craft of kintsugi, a broken cup is mended with gold, and the cracks become the most beautiful part. Learn to make, charge and burn your own seals, and keep the law of return, in The Book of Burnt Seals by S. Nonis.
