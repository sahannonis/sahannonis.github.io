The Book of Dream Keys

Of Dreams: Their Old Readers, Their Remembering, Their Asking, and the Keys That Open Them

❖

Every dream is a locked door. For more than three thousand years people have tried to read their dreams: on Egyptian papyrus and Assyrian clay, in the healing temples of Greece, in the Upanishads and the dream yoga of Tibet, and in the sleep laboratories of the last century. The Book of Dream Keys gathers that long search into a practice for tonight. Inside you will find the true history of dream reading, what science knows and does not know, seven habits for remembering, the Method of Keys for reading any dream, the ancient rite of asking a dream a question, five ways to wake inside a dream, a plain guide to nightmares, twelve Great Keys, more than sixty Lesser Keys, a Dream Ledger, and Pillow Keys to cut out and use. The key was always yours.
