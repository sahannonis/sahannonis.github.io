The Book of a Hundred and Twelve Doors

The Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra Opened for Our Time: Its Hundred and Twelve Ways into Stillness, Drawn, Explained and Practised Step by Step

❖

Between two breaths, a door. More than a thousand years ago in Kashmir, a short Sanskrit teaching set down one hundred and twelve ways into stillness: the turning of the breath, the fading of a sound, the space inside a jar, the edge of sleep, the taste of food, even the moment of a sneeze. The Book of a Hundred and Twelve Doors opens every one of them for our time. Each door is a full spread: the verse in plain words, a drawing of the practice, what it is, why it works, how to do it step by step, how to use it in daily life, and where the Bhagavad Gītā answers. Seven teachings explain the world, waking up and the map of the inner body. A forty-day path and 112 cut-out door cards help you begin. Do not collect doors. Walk through one.
