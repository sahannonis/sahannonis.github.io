The Book of Knots and Wards

Of Folk Protection: Its Thresholds and Threads, Its Knots Tied and Untied, and the Thirty Wards

❖

What is tied holds. What is untied is freed. Every people on earth has guarded its doorways and its loved ones: with iron horseshoes and daisy wheels scratched by English hearths, shoes hidden in walls, blue eyes in Turkish doorways, the hamsa, the mezuzah, the lime and chillies of Indian shops, and the white pirith thread tied on wrists in the temples of Lanka. The Book of Knots and Wards gathers that long care into wards you can make with your own hands. Inside you will find the true history of thresholds and of knot magic, a guide to cord and colour, three working knots and the knots of meaning, wards for the home and wards that are worn, the rite of untying, five honest rules, thirty illustrated wards, a Ledger of Wards, and Cord Cards and Threshold Marks to cut out. Tie it with care. Untie it with thanks.
