The Book of Burnt Seals

Of Sigils: Their Old Roots, Their Making, Their Burning, and the Law of What Returns

❖

A seal is a want given a shape. For thousands of years people have pressed their hopes into clay, cut them into copper, and drawn them on paper to be carried, buried or burnt. The Book of Burnt Seals gathers that long history, from Solomon's ring and Agrippa's magic squares to the island yantras of Lanka and the chaos magic of Austin Osman Spare, and turns it into a practice you can begin tonight. Inside you will find three ways to draw a seal, five doors for charging it, the rite of burning and the law of return, a chapter on summoning the strength within, forty-two seals for every corner of a life, and blank seals to cut out, draw and burn yourself. Intention is the root. Fire is the messenger. What is sent returns.
