The Book of Sung Runes

Of the Old Northern Letters: Their Carving and Their Singing, Their Reading, and the Twenty-Four Runes One by One

❖

Cut it with care. Stain it red. Then give it your breath. For more than a thousand years, the peoples of the North carved runes into combs, swords, standing stones and little sticks of wood, and sang charms over them. The Book of Sung Runes sets out the twenty-four runes of the oldest futhark, truly and plainly: where they came from, what the old poems say of each, and how to carve, stain, sing and read them today. Learn the science of sound that is real, from Chladni's singing sand to the humming breath, and leave behind the claims that are not. Plant an honest rune garden. Turn a bad omen into a plan. Twenty-four plates, two original tales, a song log, a garden ledger, rune cards and a casting cloth complete it. A rune is a letter cut with care. Read it as a mirror.
