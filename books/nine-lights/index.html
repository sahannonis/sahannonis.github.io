The Book of Nine Lights

Jyotiṣa, the Star-Knowledge of India: Its Roots, Its Craft and Its Road to Freedom, Set Out Accurately and in Plain Words

❖

The light that moves in the sky moves also in you. India has watched the sky for more than two thousand years, and for well over a thousand it has read the birth chart in its own way: by the nine lights, the twelve signs, the twenty-seven mansions of the Moon and the twelve houses of a life. The Book of Nine Lights sets out that whole tradition, jyotiṣa, accurately and in plain words. Learn the roots and the history; cast a chart by hand, step by step, with a worked example; time a life by the dashas; find the yogas; match two charts point by point; follow Saturn's seven and a half years; and walk the road of karma, rebirth and freedom with the Bhagavad Gītā. A full page for every light, sign and mansion, tables of the sky from 1900 to 2050, two original tales and tear-out worksheets complete it. A chart can be a mirror. It should never be a master.
