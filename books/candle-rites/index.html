The Book of Candle Rites

Of Sacred Light: Its Old Keepers, Its Colours, Its Carving and Burning, and the Rites of the Small Flame

❖

A flame asks nothing of the dark. For thousands of years people have lit small fires on purpose: Egyptian rushlights, the beeswax of medieval churches, the diyas of Diwali, the Vesak lamps of Lanka, the memorial candles of the Jewish year and the altar candles of the Days of the Dead. The Book of Candle Rites gathers that long practice into rites you can begin tonight. Inside you will find the true history of sacred light, Faraday's anatomy of a flame, a guide to waxes, shapes and colours, the arts of carving and dressing, the gaze of the yogis, the reading of flame and wax, the nine rules of fire, thirty-six illustrated rites, a Candle Log, and Candle Cards to cut out. Light it with intention. Watch it with care.
