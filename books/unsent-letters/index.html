The Book of Unsent Letters

Of Letters Never Meant to Arrive: Their Old Keepers, Their Writing, Their Burning, and the Peace They Leave

❖

Some letters are not written to be read. For four thousand years people have written to those who could never receive their words: on pottery bowls left in Egyptian tombs, in Lincoln's furious letters that were never sent, on paper burnt for the ancestors, and down a telephone in a Japanese garden connected to nowhere. The Book of Unsent Letters turns that long practice into one you can begin tonight. Inside you will find the true history of letters never meant to arrive, what research says about why writing heals, the three drafts of every honest letter, the five releases, the answering letter, letters to your younger and future self, a careful chapter on heavy letters, thirty-three guided letters, and letter papers and envelopes to cut out, write, seal and burn. Write it plainly. Write it all. Then let it burn.
