The Book of Tea Leaves

Of the Reading of Cups: Its Old Readers, Its Method, Its Symbols, and What the Leaves Show Those Who Look

❖

Drink slowly. Leave a little. Turn the cup, and look. For three centuries people have turned their cups and read the leaves: in Victorian parlours and Highland kitchens, over the thick coffee of the Ottoman world, and under the tea sheds of Lanka's hill country. The Book of Tea Leaves gathers that long custom into an art you can begin with your next cup. Inside you will find the true history of tea and of cup-reading, an honest account of why the eye finds meaning in chance, how to choose, brew and turn the cup, the map of the cup, a six-step method of reading, how to read coffee grounds, the seven promises of the honest reader, twenty-four illustrated Great Symbols, more than seventy Lesser Symbols, a Tea Ledger and Cup Maps to cut out. The leaves fall where they fall. The reading is real.
